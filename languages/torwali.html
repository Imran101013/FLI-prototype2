ISO 639-3: trw Dardic (Indo-Aryan)

Torwali

توروالی
About the language

A Dardic language of the Indo-Aryan family, Torwali is spoken in the Bahrain and Chail areas of District Swat. By some estimates the Torwali people number between 120,000 and 130,000, possibly half of them in the Swat heartland.

Rigorous work on the revitalization of Torwali has been carried out since 2004. Bahrain is the cultural and administrative centre of the language. Torwali speakers are very proud of their language and culture; although many also speak Urdu and Pashto, they use Torwali with each other, while increasingly borrowing Urdu, Pashto and English words.

Traditionally most Torwalis were subsistence farmers. Today they grow mostly corn, wheat, tomatoes, apples and pears for local markets and larger cities to the south.
