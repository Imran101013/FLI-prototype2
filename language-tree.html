Languages of Our Target Area: Northern Pakistan

The mountains of northern Pakistan are home to an extraordinary variety of languages. Explore the 29 languages FLI works with, where they are spoken, how they are related, and what has been done to document them.

Pakistan Language Tree

The languages of northern Pakistan come from several ancestral stocks. Most belong to the Indo-Aryan (Dardic) and Iranian branches of Indo-European, alongside Nuristani, the Sino-Tibetan language Balti, and the language isolate Burushaski.

Preserving the wealth of regional cultures and languages of Pakistan

Established in 2002 to assist the minoritized language communities of northern Pakistan in preserving and developing their mother tongues. We put mother-tongue speakers in the driving seat of language development and cultural documentation.
