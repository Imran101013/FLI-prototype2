Key Branches Represented in the Chart

Direct linguistic family nodes documented across the languages of Pakistan:

Indo-Aryan: Dardic Northwestern Zone

Languages: Gawri, Torwali, Palula, Indus Kohistani, Bateri, Chilisso, Gowro, Shina, Khowar, Kalasha, Dameli, Gawar-Bati, Kashmiri, Ushojo, Kundal Shahi, Kalkoti.

Phonological trait: Conserves archaic Sanskrit consonantal clusters and tone.
Iranian (Pamir & SE) Indo-Iranian

Languages: Wakhi (Gojal/Upper Chitral), Yidgha (Lutkuh, Chitral), Ormuri (Kaniguram, S. Waziristan), Munji, Pashto, Balochi.

Distribution: Pamir knot, Upper Chitral, and Sulaiman range.
Language Isolate No Known Relatives

Language: Burushaski (Hunza, Nagar, and Yasin valleys of Gilgit-Baltistan).

Four grammatical genders, vigesimal numbers, and ergative-absolutive alignment.
Sino-Tibetan Tibetic Branch

Languages: Balti (Skardu, Ghanche, Shigar, Kharmang, Rondu), Purik.

Preserves archaic Old Tibetan initial consonants lost in Modern Tibetan.
Nuristani Third Aryan Branch

Languages: Kati (Kamviri/Shekhani in Chitral), Ashkun, Waigali, Prasun, Tregami.

Split from Indo-Iranian before the Indo-Aryan/Iranian divergence.
Dravidian (North) Northern Dravidian

Language: Brahui (Kalat, Mastung, Khuzdar, Balochistan).

Geographical outlier 1,500+ km from South Indian Dravidian languages.
