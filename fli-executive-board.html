Governance

FLI Board Members

FLI is governed by an Executive Board that brings together linguists, educators, development professionals and leaders from the language communities we serve.

FA
Executive Leadership Head of the Organization

Mr. Fakhruddin Akhunzada

Executive Director • Forum for Language Initiatives • fakhruddin@fli-online.org

Executive Director of the Forum for Language Initiatives and head of the organization. He served as Conference Secretary of the 28th Conference of the Foundation for Endangered Languages (FEL XXVIII, Islamabad 2024) and is the author of “Kalkatak: A Crossroads of Cultures in Chitral” (2009) and “Languages of Chitral: Past, Present, Future” (2023) in FLI’s Language and Culture Series.

RK
Chairman

Mr. Rozi Khan Burki

Chairman of the Board
Linguist, poet and author · Former Member, Federal Board of Revenue

Rozi Khan Burki is a distinguished linguist, poet, author, and former senior civil servant who retired from the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) as a Member. Throughout his career he combined public service with a deep commitment to the preservation and promotion of Pakistan’s linguistic and cultural heritage.

Read full profileShow less

A native speaker of Ormuri, an endangered language spoken by a small community in South Waziristan, Mr. Burki has played a pioneering role in documenting, developing and promoting his mother tongue through literature, educational resources and community engagement. His publications in Ormuri help preserve its oral and literary traditions, and he has been instrumental in developing reading and learning materials for younger speakers.

FQ
Board Member

Ms. Fauzia Qazi

Member, Executive Board
Development professional · Founder, Rove Pakistan Travel & Tours

Fauzia Qazi is a seasoned development professional with 26+ years of expertise in project management, gender initiatives and entrepreneurship. She holds an MSc in Applied Development Studies from the University of Reading, UK, as a Joint Japan/World Bank Graduate Scholarship recipient, and a BSc in Agriculture from the Agriculture University, Peshawar.

Read full profileShow less

With a strong background in grassroots mobilization, women’s empowerment and natural resource management, she has worked with AKRSP, USAID, World Vision International, Hashoo Foundation, the Women Development Directorate (GB) and other NGOs. She founded Rove Pakistan Travel & Tours and has served on several boards and the Rawalpindi and Hunza Chambers of Commerce.

FA
Board Member

Dr. Farhat Abdullah

Member, Executive Board
Linguist · University of Central Punjab, Lahore

Dr. Farhat Abdullah has an active career in teaching and research spanning over a decade. In 2018 the Higher Education Commission (HEC) awarded her a Faculty Development Program Scholarship for her PhD, and she undertook a research fellowship at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

Read full profileShow less

She attended the Linguistic Society of America’s CoLang 2016 in Fairbanks, Alaska, contributing to her certification in language documentation, and worked as a linguist on the DAAD-funded Urdu WordNet project. A permanent faculty member at the University of Central Punjab since 2013, she teaches sociolinguistics, semantics, TESOL and forensic linguistics, and received the Best Faculty Teacher Award in 2021.

RK
Board Member

Mr. Razwal Kohistani

Member, Executive Board
Linguist, author and cultural researcher · Indus Kohistan

Razwal Kohistani is a renowned Pakistani linguist, author and cultural researcher from Indus Kohistan, celebrated for over four decades of pioneering work in the documentation and standardization of Kohistani Shina. His achievements include a three-volume Shina–Kohistani–Urdu Dictionary and an Arabic-based script and digital keyboard for Kohistani Shina.

Read full profileShow less

He received the Presidential Award in 2024 for a Shina-language biography of the Prophet Muhammad, and has authored or co-authored nearly 50 works in Shina, Urdu and English on phonology, grammar, sociolinguistics, oral traditions and the tribal history of Kohistan.

FK
Board Member

Mr. Fazal Karim

Member, Executive Board
Project design and monitoring & evaluation specialist

Mr. Fazal Karim Khan is a results-oriented professional with over 18 years of expertise in project design, implementation and monitoring and evaluation (M&E) of multisector projects — including civic education, livelihoods, education, democratic governance, emergency response and local government — across Pakistan, including the Newly Merged Districts and Balochistan.

Read full profileShow less

He has worked on projects funded by USAID, EU, World Bank, FCDO, IFAD and CBM, held key roles with DAI, Save the Children and Blumont, and served as an Evaluation Expert with OPM. He holds master’s degrees in social sciences and English Literature.

ZK
Board Member

Mr. Zulfiqar Khan

Member, Executive Board
Former Director, Curriculum and Teacher Education, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

With a career spanning over 31 years, Mr. Zulfiqar Khan retired as Director Curriculum and Teacher Education, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. He has contributed extensively to curriculum development from ECCE to Grade XII and ALP I–VIII, designed schemes of studies for formal and non-formal education, and led the development and review of textbooks and supplementary reading materials.

Read full profileShow less

His expertise extends to assessment tools, training manuals and modules, monitoring and evaluation, and professional development, serving as a master trainer, coordinator and planner of educational training activities.

Preserving the wealth of regional cultures and languages of Pakistan

Established in 2002 to assist the minoritized language communities of northern Pakistan in preserving and developing their mother tongues. We put mother-tongue speakers in the driving seat of language development and cultural documentation.
