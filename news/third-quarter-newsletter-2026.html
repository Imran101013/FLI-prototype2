FLI Releases 3rd Quarter Newsletter (July–September 2026)

October 2026: The Forum for Language Initiatives (FLI) has launched its 3rd Quarter Newsletter, highlighting key activities and achievements from July to September 2026.

Key Highlights

  • Teacher Workshops: Training sessions for government schoolteachers to strengthen literacy and education.
  • Community Poetry Events: Celebrating local poets and oral traditions, fostering cultural pride.
  • New Publications: Release of books and resources in multiple Pakistani languages, supporting preservation and learning.
  • Advocacy & Outreach: Strategic efforts to promote language rights and cultural heritage at regional and national levels.

Voices from the Field

The newsletter captures stories of teachers, poets, and community leaders who are working to keep Pakistan’s linguistic diversity alive. It also showcases FLI’s ongoing partnerships with grassroots organizations and cultural institutions.

Access & Engagement

The publication is available digitally and will be shared widely through FLI’s website and social media platforms. Readers are encouraged to explore the newsletter to learn more about the vibrant initiatives shaping Pakistan’s indigenous language landscape.
